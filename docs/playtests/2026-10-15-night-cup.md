## What worked

- **Setting up**: the form's hint "About 6 prompts in 30 minutes, or more if they're said quickly", the countdown, "LIVE 28:36 left", the team boxes.
- **The verb rule's messages** are clear to every agent: "(“died” counts as “die”)", "(“swimming” counts as “swim”)", "it's 7 letters, not 8 or more".
- **The notes** said what was needed, without examples (Dee: "clear and gave no examples").
- **Free repeats**: 0 on every repeat, 2 for a word that doesn't fit.
- **Teams**: team scores are the sum of their players'; the Teams tab; dots by name.
- **Removing with Remove**: "Ada Tester (removed)" kept her 72 points on the leaderboard and in Night Owls' total; her next word got "You've been removed from this competition."; she could still watch.
- **The end**: the results card above, `myResult`, My words listing every word (Ben 32, Dee 37, Cal 22), and "This competition has finished." for a word after the end.

## The verb rule in play

It works and it's explained, but it costs: most of the round's penalties were -ed and -ing forms on letter prompts, and two cases feel unfair to players:

- **Nouns that are -ing forms**: *programming*, *swimming* (refused on "double M") are words in their own right to most people. The rule treats every -ing form as its verb unless a meaning prompt names it.
- **The 2-point penalty on top**: a player who forgets the rule loses points for a "right" word several times a prompt. Options: make a form refused by the rule free (like a typo), or keep the penalty and rely on About this prompt.

**Decision needed**: whether either softening is wanted.

## Smaller points

- **`did` was accepted on "Starts and ends with D"** while *died*, *dyed*, *dried* weren't: *did*'s verb *do* is too short to be a word here, so *did* stands on its own (as *going* does). Consistent with the rules, odd to a player.
- **Place names**: *alaska* is accepted (it's in the word list), *asia*, *africa*, *arizona*, *alabama* aren't. Proper nouns shouldn't be in at all: a check of the word list for place names is worth doing.
- **Phrases missing**: *dead end*, *common sense*, *a la carte*; *bunyip*, *kickball*, *dogsled*, *gemmology*, *pommelled* (reported by the agents).
- **The mythical creature list's Nobody said** shows *monster*, *giant*, *alien*, *demon* first, which reads as if nobody tried the obvious; they're the commonest unsaid, as designed.
- **`teams[].position` in `raw` is a fixed index, not a rank** (from the Kitchen Cup report; the page uses it only for colours).
- **The page says "No penalty."** for a free repeat; the agents only see `penalty: 0` in the API.
