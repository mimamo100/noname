## For the owner

1. **Players miss the prompt changing** (Ada, at phone width): the free first wrong word helps, but the next costs 2, and on a phone the prompt sits above the word box, out of sight once the page scrolls. **Suggested**: a short flash or banner by the word box when the prompt changes ("New prompt: A kind of dog").
2. **Plurals are listed as typed on length prompts** (Ben): *rocks* on "exactly 4 letters" counts as *rock* but is listed as *rocks*, unlike *hugging* listed as *hug*. **Suggested**: list a plural as its singular when a length rule judged it so; or leave plurals as typed.
3. **Organisers can't see who's in which team on the Overall leaderboard** (Eve): only a colour dot in Just said, and no way to see who caused a team's minus score on a prompt. **Suggested**: the team's dot (or name) on the Overall tab too.
4. **Smaller page points**: under a minute left, "or in 1m" while the competition clock shows 0:16 (show seconds); after the end the Teams table still has a "This prompt" column at 0; "(+4 family)" in Just said is cryptic; "Report it: it should count" is offered even for plainly off-prompt words (*chopping board* on a dog prompt); "Remove" and "Remove completely" aren't explained in settings; the Recent prompt card repeats the last Earlier prompt (already decided: drop it while that prompt is in the list).
5. **Doubtful list entries** shown in "Nobody said" (Dee, Eve, Ada): *glass*, *cup*, *china*, *plate*, *sink*, *range* as kitchen equipment; *lied*, *mass*, *acid* as music or dance; *ghost*, *giant*, *witch* as mythical creatures (*ghost* and *witch* arguably fine). A pass over these lists' main answers is worth doing.
6. **Families across meanings** (Dee, Ben): *stock* (the plant) used up *stocked*, *trap* (the music) *trapped*, *sage* (the herb) *sager*. The known cost of families by spelling; only worth breaking where it bites.
7. **Not bugs**: the last prompt ran past 5 minutes to the end (by design: no prompt starts in a competition's last minutes); prompt numbers skip because all worlds share them; *strawberry* scored without the prompt bonus as an "also" plant (it's a fruit first).
8. **Missing**: *St John's wort* (an apostrophe phrase), *salsas*.
