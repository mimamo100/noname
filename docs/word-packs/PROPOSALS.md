# Word packs: review proposals

Review of the drafts in [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md), 13 October 2026. **Nothing has been changed yet.** Reply with anything to leave out, change or add, or "approved", plus your answers to the decisions below.

## Summary

Every category file of the three packs was read in full by a reviewer, and every proposed word checked against the pack and the blocklist. The drafts are sound: only 8 removals across all three packs. Their problem is that they're thin on the words a player reaches for first.

- **About 840 adds to main and 1,040 to also.** Each add is written as the full line with its forms (`isotope isotopes`).
- **About 190 moves up to main** (everyday answers stuck in *also*, like *testtube*, *major* and *minor*, *pick* and *neck*) and **140 down to also** (specialist terms counted as main, like *amyloplast*, *capias*, *sarod*).
- **33 form fixes**: missing plurals, UK/US spellings, alternative spellings (*amp* for *ampere*, *ukelele*, *leggero*).
- **3 label changes**: science equipment, law courts, music markings.

### The biggest gaps

- **Science**: no *DNA* or *RNA* anywhere; chemistry and physics lack the school basics (*gas*, *liquid*, *solid*, *rust*, *magnet*, *lever*); earth science has about ten rocks, almost no landforms (*valley*, *cave*) and almost no weather (*fog*, *snow*); "Something in space" has nothing made by people (*astronaut*, *rocket*, *probe*); *amp* isn't accepted as a unit.
- **Law**: the obvious courtroom words (*jury*, *witness*, *judge*, *guilty*, *innocent*) aren't in the trial category; inheritance lacks *will*, *heir* and *probate*; there's no category for criminals themselves (*murderer*, *burglar*, *conman*).
- **Music**: musicians lacks *singer* and *rapper*; theory has *major* and *minor* only in *also*; gigs lacks *show*, *performance* and *rave*; dance has no ballet steps.

## Decisions for you

1. **Phrases now that the game joins up what players type.** When we agreed that run-together phrases go in *also*, the reason was that players wouldn't think to squash "tea towel" into *teatowel*. Now the game does it for them: they type "habeas corpus" or "hip hop" naturally. Should well-known phrases now count (go in *main*)? It matters most for Latin legal phrases (*habeas corpus*, *mens rea*, *pro bono*, *bona fide*: without them the Latin category has about 21 answers), and for *hip hop*, *rock and roll*, *test tube*, *black hole*, *electric chair*. **Suggestion:** yes, phrases most players would give count; obscure ones stay in *also*. The same would apply to the everyday categories (*tea towel*, *T-shirt*, *sweet pea*), as a separate change.
2. **Abbreviations in packs.** For everyday words you ruled them out. In packs, some are the first thing anyone says: *DNA*, *RNA* (science), *GBH*, *ABH*, *ASBO*, *DUI* (law). **Suggestion:** allow well-known ones in packs; *DNA* in main, the rest in *also*.
3. **Blocked crimes in the law pack.** *Rape*, *incest*, *sodomy* and *bestiality* are blocked everywhere, so the law pack can never accept them. You decided sexual crimes stay blocked. **Suggestion:** keep it that way; the feed shows every word, and that's the same in a law world.
4. **New categories.** Reviewers suggest: **"A criminal"** for law (*murderer*, *thief*, *burglar*, *hitman*, *conman*, about 50 answers, drafted in the crime section's notes); and splitting science's earth category into **"A rock or mineral"**, **"A weather term"** and what's left (landforms and the Earth's structure). **Suggestion:** yes to all three.
5. **Names.** *Bunsen* (as in Bunsen burner) is now an ordinary word, like *newton*: suggest main. *Geiger* (counter) was left out as a name: suggest *also*. Dwarf planets: *pluto* is in; suggest *ceres* in main and *eris*, *haumea*, *makemake* in *also*, a small closed set like the planets.
6. **Small categories.** A few have too few answers for many letter rules: subatomic particles (30), singing voices (about 35), groups of musicians (about 30), courts (about 45 after changes). The game already skips prompts with too few answers, so nothing breaks; they'll just come up mostly as plain "A subatomic particle" prompts. **Suggestion:** leave them.
