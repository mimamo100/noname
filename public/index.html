    How to play
    • Type words that fit the current prompt. Some prompts are about meaning (“A bird”), some about letters (“Starts and ends with N”), and some both (“An animal starting with B”).
    • Once a word is said, nobody in this world can say it again, ever. Its family goes with it: say cat and cats is gone too.
    • Plurals count as their singular: brothers is judged and scored as brother, so it doesn't fit “8+ letters”.
    • Longer and rarer words score more. Scores rise as fewer words are left unsaid, both in the whole dictionary and in the current prompt.
    • You can save a word for later when it's worth more, but someone else might say it first.
    • Careful: a word that doesn't fit, or has already been said, costs 2 points. Typos are free, and so is a real answer missing from our lists: report it so we can add it.
    • UK and US spellings of a word (colour, color) count as one answer. Some private worlds accept only one of them.
    • The prompt changes when nothing fits any more, or when its time runs out.
