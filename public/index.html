    How to play
    • Type words that fit the current prompt. Some prompts are about meaning (“A bird”), some about letters (“Starts and ends with N”), and some both (“An animal starting with B”).
    • Once a word is said, nobody in this world can say it again, ever. Its family goes with it: say cat and cats is gone too.
    • Plurals count as their singular: brothers is judged and scored as brother, so it doesn't fit “8+ letters”. Words normally used in the plural (trousers, leggings) count as written.
    • Scoring. A word's points are its length minus 2 (at least 1; a two-word answer scores its longest word, plus 1 for each other word), times up to ×3 for rarer words, times two scarcity multipliers shown under the prompt:
      • World × rises as this world's dictionary gets used up: ×1.0 when it's new, ×2.0 when half the words are said, ×4.0 at three quarters, up to ×10.
      • Prompt × rises gently as the current prompt runs dry: ×1.0 at the start, ×1.5 when half its answers are said, up to ×2. It goes back to ×1.0 with each new prompt. Looser answers that don't bring the count down (the count says “at least”) score without it.
      The two multiply together, so later answers are worth a little more, but knowing rare words matters most.
    • You can save a word for later when it's worth more, but someone else might say it first.
    • Careful: a word that doesn't fit, or that someone else has already said, costs 2 points, and the message says why. Repeating your own word is free, and so is a word someone else said in the last minute, or that your own team said. Typos are free, and so is a real answer missing from our lists: report it so we can add it. If the prompt changes just as you send a word, it's judged against the prompt you saw.
    • Two-word answers can be typed either way: “tea towel”, “tea-towel” and “teatowel” are the same.
    • UK and US spellings of a word (colour, color) count as one answer. Some private worlds accept only one of them.
    • A prompt ends once 80% of its answers are said, or when its time runs out. Whoever says the answer that ends it gets 10 bonus points 🏁. Then Recent prompt shows what nobody said: the commonest answers left.
    • Where to play. The big world is open to everyone signed in and starts afresh every day, with quick prompts. Rooms hold up to 60 players and last until their words run out, which takes weeks. If you stop playing for a week your seat goes to someone new, but your words and points stay. Private worlds are for you and the friends you invite: anyone can join one with its invite link, and creating one needs the Host plan. Private worlds and competitions can use a word pack (Science, Law, Music…) instead of everyday words. Competitions pit teams against each other between a start and an end time: open your team's link to join it, and every point you score counts for your team. Running one needs the Organiser plan.
    • My worlds (under your name, top right) lists the rooms and private worlds you're in. My games shows how you finished in past rounds and rooms, your personal bests, and your streak: the days in a row you've said a word. Daily email, in the same menu, reminds you each morning if you haven't played yet.

    My word packs

    Your own themed words for your private worlds and competitions: a topic you teach, your company's products, a quiz theme.

      Each list starts with its prompt (“A planet”), then one word per line. Leave a blank line between lists. Put other forms of a word on the same line, separated by commas, like nucleus, nuclei or black hole, black holes, so they count as one answer. Phrases are fine on a line with commas (a phrase on its own takes a comma after it: black hole,): players type them with or without the spaces, and see them as you wrote them. Each word or phrase needs 3–30 letters, a to z (spaces, hyphens and apostrophes don't count).
